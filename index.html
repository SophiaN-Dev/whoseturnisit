Whose turn is it, anyway?

Whether you're figuring out whose turn it is to present, who's doing the dishes, or who goes first in game night, Who's it?! is a free random name picker that settles it fairly every time. Add your names, hit Pick, and let the wheel, slots, or cards decide — no sign-up, no ads, just an honest random pick.

How do I fairly decide whose turn it is?

Add everyone's name to the list and hit Pick. The result is fully random, decided the instant you press the button, so nobody can say it was rigged — the wheel spin, slot pull, or card flip you see afterward is just for fun.

Is this random name picker free?

Yes — Who's it?! is completely free to use, with no account or sign-up required.

Can I save my list of names for next time?

Yes. Save any list of names and load it again whenever you need to pick from that same group — handy for a recurring team standup or a regular game night.

About who's it?!

The idea for Who's it?! came about when it was time for presentations and we needed to find who would present. We decided to create a tool for it — and here it is!

It's free, it doesn't need an account, and your lists stay on your own device. Spin, shuffle, or flip your way to a decision, and let the wheel take the blame.

How it works

The moment you tap Pick, your browser picks a random name from your list — every name has exactly the same chance, no matter how many times you've spun before or who won last time.

The wheel spin, slot pull, or card shuffle you see afterward is just for fun — it's animated to land on the name that was already picked. It doesn't change the odds.

Everything happens on your own device. No names, picks, or lists are ever sent anywhere.

Privacy policy

Who's it?! doesn't ask for an account or any personal information to work.

The names you add are stored only in your own browser's local storage. They never get sent to us or anyone else — if you clear your browser data, they're gone for good.

We don't currently use cookies or tracking. If we add advertising in the future, our ad partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads, and we'll update this page when that happens.
